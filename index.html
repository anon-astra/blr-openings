Turkish-Mediterranean restaurant Indiranagar · opened Oct 16 2026

Kebapci

Kebapci's third Bengaluru outlet, on 100 Feet Road, built around an open fire grill and its signature Cağ Kebap.

Turkish-Mediterranean restaurant Kebapci has opened its third Bengaluru outlet on 100 Feet Road in Indiranagar, following its locations in Koramangala and Forum South and its fine-dining concept Oz at UB City. Founders Aasim Shah and Adeeb Shah said Indiranagar felt long overdue.

The headline dish is the Cağ Kebap, a lamb preparation slow-cooked on a horizontal fire grill and carved tableside, which the brand says it is the only restaurant in India to serve. The new outlet also introduces a Gerçek Adana Kebap, alongside fire-grilled meats, mezze platters and salads.

The interiors were conceptualised by Turkish architects, with the layout bringing guests closer to the open grill.

3,000 sq ft specialty coffee café with an in-house bakery and a French–Japanese all-day kitchen.

Nightclub & lounge Kasturba Road, CBD · opened Oct 9 2026

Bahati

RJ-DJ Rohit Barker's two-floor sky club with house, Latin and Afro sets.

Bahati, Swahili for 'good luck', is RJ and DJ Rohit Barker's newest venture, taking over the 17th and 18th floors of a building on Kasturba Road with views over the Cubbon Park canopy, Kingfisher Tower and Chinnaswamy Stadium.

The name comes from a blue-eyed leopard Barker and his partners spotted in the Masai Mara. The 17th floor is a low-seated lounge with soft lighting; the upper deck has the dance floor and DJ console. The music leans house, Latin and Afro, with Barker and Nikhil Chinapa on the decks for the opening weekend.

Cocktails follow the wild theme: Chui (leopard) mixes reposado tequila, mezcal, charred pineapple and Kanthari chillies, and Maridadi (elegant) pairs Scotch with coconut, rooibos and pandan. Cost for two is about ₹5,000.
