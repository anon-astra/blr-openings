Chocolate lounge and café HSR Layout · opened Oct 20 2026

SMOOR

SMOOR's 75th outlet in India, a 48-seat lounge with indoor and outdoor seating, opened as the Bengaluru-born brand turns 10.

The 2,450 sq ft ground-floor lounge is aimed at young professionals, corporate customers and residents, open from breakfast through dinner, with coffee and SMOOR's desserts alongside a full menu.

The menu mixes European techniques with Asian flavours: Hoon Twun dumplings, chicken schnitzel, panko fried prawns, a gochujang cottage cheese burger, dandan noodles and teriyaki salmon, plus breakfasts like banana pancakes and kheema pav.

Korean quick-service restaurant Rajajinagar (Orion Mall) · opened Oct 19 2026

bibigo bapsang

India's first bibigo bapsang, a Korean quick-service format from GTGO and CJ Foods, built around a build-your-own rice bowl.

GTGO, the Korean food company behind GOPIZZA, Gochujang and Dalkomi, has opened the first bibigo bapsang in India at Orion Mall, Rajajinagar, through a partnership with CJ Foods, the company behind the bibigo brand. More Bengaluru outlets are planned by the end of the year.

The signature is BYOB (Build Your Own Bapsang), a customisable bowl inspired by the Korean table with rice, protein and vegetables. The menu also has bibimbap, Korean chicken, Korean fries, corndogs and desserts, at an affordable price point.

Turkish-Mediterranean restaurant Indiranagar · opened Oct 16 2026

Kebapci

Kebapci's third Bengaluru outlet, on 100 Feet Road, built around an open fire grill and its signature Cağ Kebap.

Turkish-Mediterranean restaurant Kebapci has opened its third Bengaluru outlet on 100 Feet Road in Indiranagar, following its locations in Koramangala and Forum South and its fine-dining concept Oz at UB City. Founders Aasim Shah and Adeeb Shah said Indiranagar felt long overdue.

The headline dish is the Cağ Kebap, a lamb preparation slow-cooked on a horizontal fire grill and carved tableside, which the brand says it is the only restaurant in India to serve. The new outlet also introduces a Gerçek Adana Kebap, alongside fire-grilled meats, mezze platters and salads.

The interiors were conceptualised by Turkish architects, with the layout bringing guests closer to the open grill.

Frozen Greek yogurt & açaí bar Indiranagar · opened Oct 13 2026

Friyo

Artisanal frozen Greek yogurt and açaí soft-serve bar with in-house bases and a build-your-own toppings bar. About ₹250–450 per person.

Friyo is a new frozen Greek yogurt and açaí soft-serve bar in Indiranagar from founders Debasis Das and Subhasmita Panigrahi, built around small-batch, in-house bases rather than premixes.

Classic and chocolate frozen Greek yogurt sit alongside a vegan açaí soft-serve and a Mixed Swirl that combines both. Signature cups include Oops All Biscoff, Just Like Snickers, Cherry on Top and Glow Cup, with a toppings bar of house sauces and crunch mixes.

Specialty coffee & all-day café Indiranagar · opened Oct 13 2026

Kura

3,000 sq ft specialty coffee café with an in-house bakery and a French–Japanese all-day kitchen.

Kura, named after the Japanese word for a storehouse of treasured possessions, is a new 3,000 sq ft specialty coffee and all-day dining space in Indiranagar from first-time founders Kiran Vijayakumar and Prajwal Venugopal.

The coffee programme features Indian coffees from the Northeast, Himalayan foothills, Koraput, Araku and the Western Ghats alongside international roasters, with milk-clarified coffees, Frost blended cold brew and three-beverage coffee journeys.

The kitchen pairs French technique with Japanese precision: house-laminated viennoiserie, pastries and hand-rolled pasta, plus Eggs Benedict, seafood and New Zealand lamb rack on the all-day menu.

Nightclub & lounge Kasturba Road, CBD · opened Oct 9 2026

Bahati

RJ-DJ Rohit Barker's two-floor sky club with house, Latin and Afro sets.

Bahati, Swahili for 'good luck', is RJ and DJ Rohit Barker's newest venture, taking over the 17th and 18th floors of a building on Kasturba Road with views over the Cubbon Park canopy, Kingfisher Tower and Chinnaswamy Stadium.

The name comes from a blue-eyed leopard Barker and his partners spotted in the Masai Mara. The 17th floor is a low-seated lounge with soft lighting; the upper deck has the dance floor and DJ console. The music leans house, Latin and Afro, with Barker and Nikhil Chinapa on the decks for the opening weekend.

Cocktails follow the wild theme: Chui (leopard) mixes reposado tequila, mezcal, charred pineapple and Kanthari chillies, and Maridadi (elegant) pairs Scotch with coconut, rooibos and pandan. Cost for two is about ₹5,000.
