Pub RR Nagar (80 Feet Road) · opened Nov 5 2026

Boston Pub

A five-floor, ~12,000 sq ft pub and dining venue for about 400 guests, with rooftop seating and a basement dance floor.

Each floor is meant for a different mood: indoor dining and a pub for relaxed evenings, a rooftop for sitting out, and a dance floor downstairs. Interiors by Vinay Agnelo F. D'Almeida mix vintage details with contemporary touches, and DJ nights, live music and themed events are planned.

Food is built for sharing across Asian and Korean-inspired dishes, Indian appetisers, pizzas and Continental plates, with vegetarian options throughout. Signature cocktails sit alongside non-alcoholic drinks like Ruby Fizz and Baklava Brew. Founded by Subbaiah, Preetham, Hem and Ashwin.
